Solo AgentGateway & Ambient Multicluster Mesh hosted on Kind

TO
Tom O'Rourke
EMEA Field CTO · Solo.io

Two kind clusters (east + west) running Istio Ambient with Solo agentgateway as the ingress on each cluster — AI traffic routing, tool-call guardrails, and observability across a multicluster mesh. No cloud account needed.

agentgateway Istio Ambient ztunnel MetalLB kind multicluster
