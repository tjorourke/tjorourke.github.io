Why this series

Agentic AI workloads bring a new set of failure modes — prompt injection, supply-chain rug-pulls, runaway tool calls — that live below the model layer and need infrastructure defences to match. Multicluster environments add their own complexity: cross-cluster identity, network segmentation, and policy that holds across trust boundaries.

Each demo here is a concrete, runnable answer to one of those questions.
